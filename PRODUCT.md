# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user choice).

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, engineers and collaborators checking out Aviraj Ghatora, usually from a link on a resume, LinkedIn or GitHub. They want to know quickly who he is, what he has built, where he has worked and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Aviraj Ghatora, a software developer interested in AI, data engineering and technology. It succeeds when a visitor remembers it after closing the tab and gets in touch.

## Positioning

Not a template developer portfolio: the site is an artifact in its own right, a lost ship's terminal from a 90s space-western anime, which shows his taste and craft.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Sections: landing, about, projects (bounty files), experience (mission timeline), skills (arsenal), contact (incoming transmission). All links must work. Fully responsive. Respect reduced motion.

## Brand Commitments

CLAUDE.md is binding: Cowboy Bebop-inspired retro space look, no purple, approved retro display fonts, anti-vibe-code checklist, verification at required widths. References: cowboy-bebop-anime-characters.webp (height-chart lineup), asimov-s-bounty-in-asteroid-blues.avif (bounty file screen), artefakt.mov screenshot (black, dot-matrix logo, tiny mono header grid), transmission-01.mikestaniforth.com (CRT boot and treatment). ## Product Principles

- Atmosphere serves readability, never the reverse.
- Real details only; gaps are marked, not filled.
- Every section gets its own treatment inside one world.
